## Decisions

1. Replace the default entrance + five separate personal widgets with one continuous scroll-driven identity journey. Preserve their source and data for future reuse.
2. Actual perspective camera travel, authored thick geometry, close foreground passes, a deeper destination and reversible assembly changes.
3. Keep large typography. Move its scale, clipping and position in relation to the environment rather than repeatedly changing a colored rectangle.
4. Remove clicking as the required verb in the personal narrative. Wheel, touch and keyboard scrolling are enough. Provide a direct route to Work.
5. Keep Work/Now/contact useful and honest. These quiet sections should not compete with the main motion sequence.
