# Experience repair — 2026-10-09

## What changed

- The black-hole viewport stays fixed during the bridge, so a fast wheel/trackpad gesture cannot scroll the rendered scene out of view before its animation catches up. Returning to the root resets scroll to the start. The disk's flow time scale is raised from 2.2 to 3.5; the actual camera approach still progresses gradually. A WebGL failure now keeps a scrollable image-based fallback and reaches the room rather than jumping directly to the portfolio.
- System reduced motion still applies. A visible full-motion override appears in the reduced opening; guided desktop room visitors can explicitly reopen the room with free roam. The default root does not silently ignore a visitor's accessibility preference.
- Guided room travel follows a slower center-biased camera path instead of a direct fast line. Inspections show a short observation about the physical object, not a portfolio explanation. The window now includes a shallow physical exterior rail and slowly moving rain on the glass, with the existing licensed panorama kept as the distant horizon.
- Blender regenerated the shelf maquette with distinct walls, dark rooftops, inset windows, street marks, and an intentionally missing block. The shell grew from 594,512 to 738,368 bytes; total optimized room assets are 3,471,120 bytes. No new downloaded asset or third-party license was added.
- The maquette's new meshes are explicitly part of the shelf target, so the extra detail cannot intercept inspection rays and make the shelf impossible to select.
- Touch-only rooms retain the reliable guided mode but offer optional free roam: hold Forward/Back and drag the room to look. It reuses the existing collision model and can return to guided views. Desktop pointer lock and notebook/monitor handoff remain unchanged.
- The site's second photograph now sits in a muted-sage interlude with the more literal line “Away from the desk.” Both approved portraits can expand into a full-screen image and reverse into place. Image meshes gain stronger but bounded depth. The next media source is prefetched ahead of its transition. The traveling-media effect is disabled on narrow screens after visual QA found it could collapse to a near-invisible strip there; native images remain visible instead.
- The follow-up editorial pass replaces that generic photo line with Aditya's stated interests in games, anime, and films, and how trying their small ideas makes new projects fun. Work transitions toward a warm orange rather than charcoal, with dark readable type. The About photograph no longer masks away the first word of its heading; the unused occlusion calculations are removed.

## Verification and limits

`scripts/experience-polish-qa.mjs` verifies progressive rendered black-hole frames, the fixed viewport, both photo viewers, explicit full motion, desktop free roam, and touch free roam. Room regression covers desktop walking/collision, inspection, monitor handoff, mobile guided exploration, and the accessible fallbacks. `scripts/elevation-flow-qa.mjs` covers desktop/laptop media handoffs and mobile readable-image fallback. Screenshots and recordings live in `visual-qa/experience-polish-final/`, `visual-qa/experience-polish-room/`, and `visual-qa/experience-polish-flow/`.

The room is still a small WebGL scene, not photoreal: the shelf maquette remains obviously a model; the window still uses a distant 360° image; the new rail adds parallax but not a complete exterior world. The website's media gain depth and reversible viewing, yet its page structure remains editorial and largely vertical. These are honest remaining art-direction limits, not performance bugs. The soundtrack was not replaced in this pass. Emulated browser checks are not physical-phone or subjective music review.
